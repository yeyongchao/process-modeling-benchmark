# Example 8.4 (Batch Reactor Example with Control Bounds). 


## Input

Consider a nonisothermal batch reactor with first order parallel reactions $A \rightarrow B, A \rightarrow C$, where the goal is again to find a temperature profile that maximizes the final amount of product $B$. However, here the temperature profile has an upper bound. Define the transformed temperature control and kinetic parameters as

$$u(t)=k_{10}\exp\left(-\frac{E_1}{RT(t)}\right), \qquad k=\frac{k_{20}}{k_{10}}, \qquad \beta=\frac{E_2}{E_1}.$$

The upper temperature bound maps to $0\leq u(t)\leq U$, where $U$ is the transformed upper bound. Use $k=0.5$, $\beta=2$, $U=5$, and the time horizon $0\leq t\leq t_f$ with $t_f=1$. The optimal control problem can be stated as

## Output

### 1. Variables

$a(t)$: Concentration of reactant $A$ at time $t$

$b(t)$: Concentration of product $B$ at time $t$

$u(t)$: Redefined control profile (transformed temperature) at time $t$

### 2. Objective Function

Maximize the final amount of product $B$, formulated as minimizing its negative at the final time $t_f$:

$$\min -b(t_f)$$

### 3. Constraints

Process Model (Differential Equations):

$$\frac{da}{dt} = -a(t)(u(t) + ku(t)^\beta)$$

$$\frac{db}{dt} = a(t)u(t)$$

Initial Conditions:

$$a(0) = 1$$

$$b(0) = 0$$

Control Variable Bounds:

$$u(t) \in [0, U], \qquad 0\leq t\leq t_f$$
