# Example 9.4



## Input

For the nonisothermal batch reactor with first order parallel reactions $A \rightarrow B$, $A \rightarrow C$, we maximize the final amount of product $B$ subject to an upper temperature bound over $t \in [0,1]$. Define the transformed temperature control and kinetic parameters by

$$u(t)=k_{10}\exp\left(-\frac{E_1}{RT(t)}\right), \qquad k=\frac{k_{20}}{k_{10}}, \qquad \beta=\frac{E_2}{E_1},$$

and use $U=5$, $k=0.5$, and $\beta=2$. For the sequential dynamic optimization formulation, represent the control by a constant $u_l$ on each period $l=1,\ldots,N_T$, with the period durations $h_l$ also treated as decision variables. Use the time transformation

$$t=t_{l-1}+h_l(\tau-(l-1)), \qquad \tau\in(l-1,l].$$

## Output

### 1. Variables

$a(t)$: Concentration of reactant $A$ at time $t$

$b(t)$: Concentration of product $B$ at time $t$

$u(t)$: Control profile (transformed temperature) at time $t$

$u_l$: Piecewise-constant transformed temperature control in period $l$

$h_l$: Duration of period $l$

### 2. Objective Function

Maximize the final amount of product $B$ at $t=1$, formulated as minimizing its negative:

$$\min -b(1)$$

### 3. Constraints

Process Model (Differential Equations):

$$\frac{da}{dt} = -a(t)(u(t) + ku(t)^\beta)$$

$$\frac{db}{dt} = a(t)u(t)$$

Initial Conditions:

$$a(0) = 1$$

$$b(0) = 0$$

Control Variable Bounds:

$$u(t) \in [0, U]$$

In the multiperiod sequential formulation, for each $l=1,\ldots,N_T$ the transformed dynamics are

$$\frac{da}{d\tau}=-h_la(\tau)(u_l+ku_l^\beta)$$

$$\frac{db}{d\tau}=h_la(\tau)u_l,$$

with state continuity between periods and

$$0\leq u_l\leq U, \qquad 0\leq h_l\leq\frac{2}{N_T}, \qquad \sum_{l=1}^{N_T}h_l=1.$$

The source initialization is $u_l^0=1$ and $h_l^0=1/N_T$.
