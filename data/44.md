
**Dataset Item 4 (Exercise 19.6)**

## Input

The system consists of a feed (Naphtha) being split into three final products across two stages. To solve this, we must balance the flow rates, respect the equipment capacities, and satisfy market demands while maximizing the net gain.

![fige19-7](figures/44_pd_Exercise19_6_1.png)


## Output

### 1. Variables

- $x_1$: Feed rate to Column 1 
- $x_2$: Bottoms flow from Column 1 
- $x_3$: Light ends flow from Column 1
- $x_4$: Medium solvent flow from Column 2
- $x_5$: Heavy solvent flow from Column 2

### 2. Objective Function

$$\text{Maximize } Z = -42x_1 + 50x_3 + 70x_4 + 40x_5$$

### 3. Constraints

Column 1:
$$x_3 = 0.4x_1$$ $$x_2 = 0.6x_1$$

Column 2:
$$x_4 + x_5 = x_2$$ $$0.5x_2 \le x_4 \le 0.7x_2$$ $$0.3x_2 \le x_5 \le 0.5x_2$$

Market and Capacity Limits:
$$x_1 \le 2000$$ $$x_4 \le 400$$
