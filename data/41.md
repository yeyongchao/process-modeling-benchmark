
**Dataset Item 8 (Exercise 19.13)**

## Input

A dynamic model of a continuous-flow, biological chemostat has the form $\dot{X} = 0.063C - DX$, $\dot{C} = 0.9S[X - C] - 0.7C - DC$, and $\dot{S} = -0.9S[X - C] + D$, where $X$ is the biomass concentration, $S$ is the substrate concentration, and $C$ is a metabolic intermediate concentration. The dilution rate, $D$, is an independent variable, which is defined to be the flow rate divided by the chemostat volume. Formulate a continuous optimization model to determine the value of $D$ which maximizes the steady-state production rate of biomass, $f$, given by $f = DX$.

## Output

### 1. Variables

$D$: Dilution rate

$X$: Biomass concentration

$C$: Metabolic intermediate concentration

$S$: Substrate concentration

### 2. Objective Function

Maximize the steady-state production rate of biomass:

$$\max f = DX$$

### 3. Constraints

Assuming steady-state behavior, the rate of change for all concentrations must equal zero:

$$0.063C - DX = 0$$

$$0.9S(X - C) - 0.7C - DC = 0$$

$$-0.9S(X - C) + D(10 - S) = 0$$

All physical concentrations and the dilution rate must be non-negative:

$$D, X, C, S \ge 0$$
