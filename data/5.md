### Problem: Optimal Stable Operation of an Exothermic CSTR

## Input

A first-order exothermic reaction ($A \to P$) takes place in a liquid-phase continuous stirred tank reactor (CSTR). According to Section 8.3 on temperature control, reactor operation must satisfy both heat-removal requirements and thermal stability conditions. The reactor temperature is maintained at the setpoint $T^{o}=200^\circ C$ and the conversion depends on the operating condition through the relation  $\xi = f(T^{o}, \frac{V}{F})$. For the considered setpoint, the coolant temperature must be selected to ensure thermal stability, while the residence time affects the achievable conversion. The objective is to maximize the conversion while preserving stable temperature control. 


## Output

### 1. Variables

- $\tau = \frac{V}{F}$: Residence time
- $T_{ag}$: Coolant temperature
- $\xi$: Reactor conversion

Here $k=k(T^o)$ is the first-order rate constant at the fixed setpoint. The heat-transfer coefficient and area are denoted by $K_T$ and $A_T$, while $\rho$, $C_P$, $C_{A0}$, and $\Delta H_r<0$ denote the liquid density, heat capacity, feed concentration, and reaction enthalpy.


### 2. Objective Function

$$\max  \xi = \frac{k\tau}{1 + k\tau}$$


### 3. Constraints

Steady-state heat balance:
$$F\rho C_PT_{in}-\Delta H_rF\xi C_{A0}=F\rho C_PT^o+K_TA_T(T^o-T_{ag})$$

Let $(T_{ref}^o,\xi_{ref})$ be a specified neighboring operating point used to estimate the local steady-state sensitivity. Thermal stability requires the heat-removal slope to exceed the heat-generation slope; equivalently, define
$$S_{max}=\frac{F\rho C_P+K_TA_T}{{|\Delta H_r|FC_{A0}}}.$$

Steady-state thermal sensitivity:
$$\left( \frac{d\xi}{dT^o} \right)_{\text{steady}} \approx \frac{\xi - \xi_{ref}}{T^o-T_{ref}^o} \le S_{max}$$

Dynamic thermal sensitivity:
$$\left( \frac{d\xi}{dT^o} \right)_{\text{dynamic}}= \frac{1}{1-\xi} \cdot \left( \frac{d\xi}{dT^o} \right)_{\text{steady}} \le S_{max}$$

Bounds:
$$0 \le \xi < 1$$ 

$$(V/F)_{min} \le V/F \le (V/F)_{max}$$

$$T_{ag}^{min} \le T_{ag} \le T_{ag}^{max} $$
