
### Problem: Optimal Control of a Binary Distillation Column

## Input

A first-order exothermic reaction ($A \to P$) takes place in a liquid-phase continuous stirred tank reactor (CSTR). According to Section 8.3 on temperature control, reactor operation must satisfy both heat-removal requirements and thermal stability conditions. The reactor temperature is maintained at the setpoint $T^{o}=200^\circ C$ and the conversion depends on the operating condition through the relation  $\xi = f(T^{o}, \frac{V}{F})$. For the considered setpoint, the coolant temperature must be selected to ensure thermal stability, while the residence time affects the achievable conversion. The objective is to maximize the conversion while preserving stable temperature control. 


## Input

### 1. Variables

- $\tau = \frac{V}{F}$: Residence time


### 2. Objective Function

$$\max  \xi = \frac{k\tau}{1 + k\tau}$$


### 3. Constraints

Steady-state thermal sensitivity:
$$\left( \frac{d\xi}{dT^o} \right)_{\text{steady}} = \frac{\xi - \xi_{ref}}{T^o-T_{ref}^o} \le S_{max, steady}$$

Dynamic thermal sensitivity:
$$\left( \frac{d\xi}{dT^o} \right)_{\text{dynamic}}= \frac{1}{1-\xi} \cdot \left( \frac{d\xi}{dT^o} \right)_{\text{steady}} \le S_{max, dynamic}$$

Bounds:
$$0 \le \xi < 1$$ 

$$(V/F)_{min} \le V/F \le (V/F)_{max}$$

$$T_{ag}^{min} \le T_{ag} \le T_{ag}^{max} $$
