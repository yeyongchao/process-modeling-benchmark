
### Problem: Optimization of a Fermentor

## Input

Consider a fermentor, operated at constant volume, in which a single, rate-limiting substrate promotes biomass growth and product formation. Maximize product yield.


## Output

### 1. Variables

- $D$ (Dilution rate)


### 2. Objective Function

$$\max_{D} \quad J = D \cdot P$$

### 3. Constraints

Steady-State : 
$$-DX + \mu(S, P)X = 0$$ $$D(S_f - S) - \frac{1}{Y_{X/S}}\mu(S, P)X = 0$$ $$-DP + [\alpha \mu(S, P) + \beta]X = 0$$

Kinetic Constraint:
$$\mu(S, P) = \frac{\mu_m (1 - P/P_m) S}{K_m + S + S^2/K_i}$$

Physical Bounds:
$$0 \le D < D_{max}$$ $$X, S, P \ge 0$$
