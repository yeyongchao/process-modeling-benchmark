### Problem: Optimization of a Fermentor

## Input

Consider a continuous fermentor, operated at constant volume $V$, in which a single, rate-limiting substrate promotes biomass growth and product formation. The feed contains substrate at concentration $S_f$ and no biomass or product. Choose the dilution rate $D=F/V$ and the resulting steady-state concentrations $X$, $S$, and $P$ to maximize the volumetric product productivity $DP$ (product concentration times dilution rate), not a dimensionless product yield.

At steady state, biomass grows at the inhibited specific rate

$$\mu(S,P)=\frac{\mu_m(1-P/P_m)S}{K_m+S+S^2/K_i},$$

and product formation follows $[\alpha\mu(S,P)+\beta]X$. Here, $X$, $S$, and $P$ are the biomass, residual-substrate, and product concentrations; $Y_{X/S}>0$ is the biomass-on-substrate yield coefficient; $\alpha\ge0$ and $\beta\ge0$ are the growth-associated and non-growth-associated product coefficients; and $\mu_m>0$, $P_m>0$, $K_m>0$, and $K_i>0$ are kinetic parameters. The prescribed operating limit is $D_{max}>0$, and $S_f>0$. Require $0\le D<D_{max}$, $X\ge0$, $0\le S\le S_f$, and $0\le P\le P_m$.


## Output

### 1. Variables

- $D$ (Dilution rate)
- $X$ (Steady-state biomass concentration)
- $S$ (Steady-state residual-substrate concentration)
- $P$ (Steady-state product concentration)


### 2. Objective Function

$$\max_{D,X,S,P} \quad J = D \cdot P$$

### 3. Constraints

Steady-State : 
$$-DX + \mu(S, P)X = 0$$
$$D(S_f - S) - \frac{1}{Y_{X/S}}\mu(S, P)X = 0$$
$$-DP + [\alpha \mu(S, P) + \beta]X = 0$$

Kinetic Constraint:
$$\mu(S, P) = \frac{\mu_m (1 - P/P_m) S}{K_m + S + S^2/K_i}$$

Physical Bounds:
$$0 \le D < D_{max}$$
$$X \ge 0, \qquad 0 \le S \le S_f, \qquad 0 \le P \le P_m$$
