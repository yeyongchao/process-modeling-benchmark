**Dataset Item 6 (Exercise 19.8)**


## Input

A batch reactor converts component A into B, which in turn decomposes into C: $A \to B \to C$. The concentrations of A and B are denoted by $x_1$ and $x_2$, respectively. The reactor model is described by the differential equations: $\frac{dx_1}{dt} = -k_{10}x_1 e^{-E_1/RT}$ and $\frac{dx_2}{dt} = k_{10}x_1 e^{-E_1/RT} - k_{20}x_2 e^{-E_2/RT}$. Thus, the ultimate values of $x_1$ and $x_2$ depend on the reactor temperature as a function of time. The kinetic parameters are given as: $k_{10} = 1.335 \times 10^{10} \text{ min}^{-1}$, $k_{20} = 1.149 \times 10^{17} \text{ min}^{-1}$, $E_1 = 75,000 \text{ J/g mol}$, $E_2 = 125,000 \text{ J/g mol}$, and $R = 8.31 \text{ J/(g mol K)}$. The initial concentrations are $x_{10} = 0.7 \text{ mol/L}$ and $x_{20} = 0$. Formulate a continuous optimization model to find the constant temperature that maximizes the amount of B for a batch time of $0 \le t \le 6 \text{ min}$.

## Output

### 1. Variables

$T$: Constant reactor operating temperature (K)

### 2. Objective Function

Maximize the final concentration of component B at $t = 6$ minutes:

$$\max x_2(t=6)$$

### 3. Constraints

The system must satisfy the non-linear ordinary differential equations for the reaction kinetics:

$$\frac{dx_1}{dt} = -k_1 x_1$$

$$\frac{dx_2}{dt} = k_1 x_1 - k_2 x_2$$

Where the rate constants strictly follow the Arrhenius temperature dependencies:

$$k_1 = 1.335 \times 10^{10} e^{-75000/(8.31 \cdot T)}$$

$$k_2 = 1.149 \times 10^{17} e^{-125000/(8.31 \cdot T)}$$

Subject to the initial boundary conditions at $t=0$:

$$x_1(0) = 0.7$$

$$x_2(0) = 0$$
