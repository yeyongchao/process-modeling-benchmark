
### Problem: Optimization of Conversion in a Continuous Stirred-Tank Reactor (CSTR) Cascade

## Input

This problem involves modeling and optimizing a series of $N$ CSTRs. The goal is to determine the optimal operating conditions (such as temperature or residence time) to maximize the conversion of reactant A.

![alt text](figures/07_ced_Section3_2_6_1.png)

## Input

### 1. Variables

- $T_{jn}$: Cooling/Heating jacket temperature for each stage ($K$).
- $F$: Volumetric flow rate ($m^3/s$).

### 2. Objective Function

$$\max \quad J = \frac{C_{A0} - C_{An, \text{exit}}}{C_{A0}}$$

### 3. Constraints

Steady-State Material Balance:
$$0 = F(C_{An-1} - C_{An}) + r_{An} V_n$$Steady-State Energy Balance:
$$0 = F \rho c_p (T_{n-1} - T_n) + (-\Delta H_{rxn}) r_{An} V_n + U_n A_n (T_{jn} - T_n)$$

Bounds:
Temperature safety limits: $T_{min} \le T_n \le T_{max}$
Heat exchange capacity: $Q_n = U_n A_n (T_{jn} - T_n)$.
