### Problem: Optimal PID Tuning for a Blending Composition Control System

## Input

Consider the constant-volume, perfectly mixed blending system. A feedback control system is used to reduce the effect of disturbances in feed composition $x_1$ on the controlled variable, product composition, $x$. Inlet flow rate $w_2$ can be manipulated, while $w_1$ and $x_2$ are constant. Given the PI controller parameters $K_c=1.92$ and $\tau_I=4.71\ \mathrm{min}$ obtained from the source's IMC tuning, determine the derivative time that gives the improved closed-loop response reported by the source solution.

![Fig. 12.10](figures/40_pd_Exercise12_10_1.png)

The source data are $\bar w_1=650\ \mathrm{kg/min}$, $\bar w_2=350\ \mathrm{kg/min}$, $\bar x_1=0.20$, $x_2=0.60$, and $\bar x=x_{set}=0.34$. The cylindrical tank diameter is $2\ \mathrm{m}$, its nominal liquid height is $1.5\ \mathrm{m}$, and $\rho=1000\ \mathrm{kg/m^3}$. Starting from this nominal steady state, compare the response to a step $x_1:0.20\to0.40$ and the response to a $-0.10$ set-point step. Simulate each response from the step time until its transient has settled; the exercise does not prescribe a finite horizon or an integral-error index. The composition measurement has a one-minute delay (the source also separately tests a three-minute delay).

## Output

### 1. Variables

- $\tau_D \ge 0$ (min): Derivative time of the PID controller; $K_c$ and $\tau_I$ remain fixed at their given values



### 2. Objective Function

Select $\tau_D$ by comparing the simulated closed-loop transients for the specified disturbance and set-point tests. The source defines no ITAE objective or finite optimization horizon; its reported improved response is obtained with

$$\tau_D=0.4\ \mathrm{min}.$$

### 3. Constraints

Control Law:

$$w_2(t) =  K_c \left[ e(t) + \frac{1}{\tau_I} \int_0^t e(t)dt + \tau_D \frac{de(t)}{dt} \right]$$

Error Definition:

$$e(t) = x_{set}(t) - x_m(t),\qquad x_m(t)=x(t-1\ \mathrm{min})$$

Process dynamic constraint:
$$ \rho V \frac{dx(t)}{dt} = {w_1 x_1(t) + w_2(t) x_2 - (w_1 + w_2(t)) x(t)}$$

with constant nominal volume

$$V=\pi(1\ \mathrm{m})^2(1.5\ \mathrm{m}),$$

and nominal steady-state initial conditions

$$x(0^-)=0.34,\quad w_2(0^-)=350\ \mathrm{kg/min},\quad x_1(0^-)=0.20.$$

For the disturbance test, $x_{set}=0.34$ and $x_1(t)=0.20+0.20H(t)$; for the set-point test, $x_1=0.20$ and $x_{set}(t)=0.34-0.10H(t)$, where $H(t)$ is the unit step.

Asymptotic Stability:
$$\lim_{t \to \infty} e(t) = 0$$
