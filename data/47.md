**Dataset Item 20 (Exercise 20.7)**

## Input

Consider the unconstrained, single-input single-output (SISO) version of Model Predictive Control (MPC) where the control law minimizes a quadratic objective function. Suppose that the controller is designed so that the control horizon is $M = 1$ and the weighting matrices are $Q = I$ and $R = 1$. The prediction horizon $P$ can be chosen arbitrarily. Formulate the analytical optimization model that demonstrates the resulting MPC controller gain matrix has a simple analytical form.

## Output

### 1. Variables

$\Delta U(k)=\Delta u(k)\in\mathbb{R}$: The single future control move because $M=1$

$\hat E^0(k+1)\in\mathbb{R}^{P}$: The free-response tracking-error vector over predictions $k+1,\ldots,k+P$

$S=[S_1\;S_2\;\cdots\;S_P]^T\in\mathbb{R}^{P\times1}$: The dynamic vector whose $S_i$ is the unit-step response coefficient at prediction step $i$, for $i=1,\ldots,P$

### 2. Objective Function

The predicted tracking-error vector is affine in the control move:

$$\hat E(k+1)=\hat E^0(k+1)-S\Delta u(k)$$

Thus, minimize the unconstrained MPC quadratic cost function representing the predicted tracking error and control effort:

$$\min_{\Delta u(k)} J = [\hat E^0(k+1)-S\Delta u(k)]^TQ[\hat E^0(k+1)-S\Delta u(k)]+\Delta u(k)^TR\Delta u(k)$$

Setting the derivative with respect to $\Delta u(k)$ to zero gives

$$\Delta u(k)=K_c\hat E^0(k+1),$$

where the analytical controller gain is

$$K_c = (S^T Q S + R)^{-1} S^T Q$$

### 3. Constraints

The optimization is unconstrained; the following are fixed tuning parameters for a SISO system ($m=r=1$):

$$M = 1$$

$$Q = I$$

$$R = 1$$

With $S^TS=\sum_{i=1}^{P}S_i^2$, the $1\times P$ controller gain simplifies to the exact vector projection:

$$
K_c =
\frac{1}{1+\sum_{i=1}^{P} S_i^2}
\begin{bmatrix}
S_1 & S_2 & \cdots & S_P
\end{bmatrix}
$$
