
**Dataset Item 20 (Exercise 20.7)**

## Input

Consider the unconstrained, single-input single-output (SISO) version of Model Predictive Control (MPC) where the control law minimizes a quadratic objective function. Suppose that the controller is designed so that the control horizon is $M = 1$ and the weighting matrices are $Q = I$ and $R = 1$. The prediction horizon $P$ can be chosen arbitrarily. Formulate the analytical optimization model that demonstrates the resulting MPC controller gain matrix has a simple analytical form.

## Output

### 1. Variables

$\Delta U(k)$: Vector of future continuous control moves over the control horizon $M$

### 2. Objective Function

Minimize the unconstrained MPC quadratic cost function representing the predicted tracking error and control effort:

$$\min J = \hat{E}^0(k+1)^T Q \hat{E}^0(k+1) + \Delta U(k)^T R \Delta U(k)$$

Which, through exact matrix inversion, analytically yields the generic controller gain matrix:

$$K_c = (S^T Q S + R)^{-1} S^T Q$$

### 3. Constraints

The optimization is strictly constrained by the specific tuning parameters for a SISO system ($m=r=1$):

$$M = 1$$

$$Q = I$$

$$R = 1$$

Under these specific constraints, the algebraic solution for the controller gain must simplify to the exact vector projection:

$$
K_c =
\frac{1}{1+\sum_{i=1}^{P} S_i^2}
\begin{bmatrix}
S_1 & S_2 & \cdots & S_P
\end{bmatrix}
$$
